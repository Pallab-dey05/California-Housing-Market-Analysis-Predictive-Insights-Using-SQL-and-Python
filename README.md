# California-Housing-Market-Analysis-Predictive-Insights-Using-SQL-and-Python

# Overview
Performed end-to-end **housing market data analysis** using **PostgreSQL and Python**, applying **exploratory data analysis (EDA), geospatial analysis, and statistical visualization** to uncover pricing trends and market patterns.

Generated **business intelligence insights** on key housing price drivers, market segmentation, and location-based valuation to support **data-driven real estate decision-making**.

# Objectives
* Performed **end-to-end housing market data analysis** using **SQL and Python** to identify pricing trends, regional disparities, and key real estate market drivers.

* Applied **exploratory data analysis (EDA), geospatial segmentation, and statistical visualization** to examine how income, population, and housing characteristics influence property values.

* Generated **data-driven business intelligence insights** to support informed decision-making in **real estate market analysis and housing affordability evaluation**.

* Leveraged analytics to highlight **location-based inequalities and market accessibility**, contributing to more transparent, balanced, and socially responsible housing strategies.
